Welcome to AI Health Assistant!

Your intelligent companion for health analysis and medical guidance

🔍

Step 1: Describe Symptoms

Type or speak your symptoms in detail. For example: "fever and severe headache" or "persistent cough for 3 days". The more details you provide, the better our AI can assist you.

🎤

Step 2: Voice Input

Use voice recognition to describe your symptoms hands-free. Works best on localhost or secure connections. Available in English, हिन्दी, and मराठी.

🧠

Step 3: AI Analysis

Our advanced zero-shot learning AI analyzes your symptoms and provides possible conditions, recommended specialists, and suggested medical tests.

📋

Step 4: Medical History

Fill in your triage table with previous doctor visits, medications, and upload medical documents for comprehensive health tracking.

📍

Step 5: Find Doctors

Locate nearby clinics and hospitals using OpenStreetMap data. Get directions to recommended healthcare providers in your area.

🌍

Step 6: Multi-Language

Choose your preferred language from English, हिन्दी, or मराठी for a personalized experience.
